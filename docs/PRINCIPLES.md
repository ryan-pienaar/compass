# From principles to features

How each idea from Habits 1–3 shows up in Compass. All wording is our own summary of the principles, not quotation.

## Habit 1: Be proactive (principles of personal vision)

| Principle | Where in Compass |
|---|---|
| Between stimulus and response lies the freedom to choose | **Capture → pause & choose** (`N`): before anything lands on your list you answer *important?* and *urgent?*, then pick a response. "Something came up?" on Today frames interruptions the same way. |
| Responsibility = the ability to choose your response; reactive language gives it away | **Language coach** (`shared/language.ts`): gentle hints under captures, notes, reflections and concerns, with one-click proactive rewrites. Practice area: *Influence → Language*. Configurable in Settings. |
| Circle of Concern vs Circle of Influence | **Influence → Concerns**: concerns sorted into direct control (my behavior), indirect control (others' behavior) and no control (the past, situations), with a diagram whose inner circle grows as you act. |
| Every kind of problem has a first step inside your influence (change a habit, change your method of influence, change your attitude) | Each concern gets an approach (habit, influence method, or accepting response) and a **first step that becomes a Quadrant II task**. |
| "Haves" vs "bes" | Each concern has an **"I can be…"** reframe, highlighted on the card. |
| Taking initiative (resourcefulness and initiative) | Quadrant II actions everywhere: schedule it, make it a big rock, prevent the recurring crisis. |
| Mistakes: acknowledge, correct and learn from them immediately | **Evening reflection** prompt "Any mistake to acknowledge, correct and learn from?"; journal. |
| Making and keeping commitments is the heart of proactivity; integrity | Weekly rocks are **promises**; the review records how each was kept; **promises kept** shows on review and Insights. |
| The 30-day test | **Influence → 30-day test**: one small commitment a day (set on Today), an evening check-in (kept? in my circle? proactive language? owned mistakes?), and a 30-cell grid. |

## Habit 2: Begin with the end in mind (principles of personal leadership)

| Principle | Where in Compass |
|---|---|
| Envision the end of your life as the frame for every day (the funeral visualization) | **Compass → Exercises → The end in mind**: four speakers (family, friend, work, community) × character, contribution, achievement; the answers feed the mission editor as raw material. |
| All things are created twice (mental, then physical creation) | Long-term goals and tasks have an **"end in mind"** field (what done looks like); goals also have a measure (how you'll know). |
| Leadership (right things) before management (things right) | The weekly ritual starts with **Review** and **Compass** steps before any scheduling. |
| Rescripting: live out of imagination, not memory | **Affirmations** and visualization guidance; active affirmations rotate on Today. |
| Personal mission statement = personal constitution; many drafts; review regularly; the process matters | **Compass → Mission**: autosaving editor, one version per day with restore, "last reviewed" (planning counts as reviewing), role outline helper, guidance. |
| Ways to write it | 15-minute **free-write** (self-awareness, conscience, imagination), **questions for a quiet hour**, collected **quotes & ideas**. |
| Whatever is at your center becomes your security, guidance, wisdom and power | **Compass → Your center**: an honest rating of eleven possible centers plus reflection on the four life-support factors; history. |
| Affirmation ingredients: personal, positive, present tense, visual, emotional | **Affirmation builder** with a live five-ingredient check (`shared/affirmation.ts`). |
| Roles and long-term goals give the mission balance and structure; goals describe results, not activities | **Roles & goals**: role statements ("who I want to be in this role"), long-term goals with end in mind, measure, target date and progress from linked weekly rocks; an activity-sounding goal gets a nudge. |

## Habit 3: Put first things first (principles of personal management)

| Principle | Where in Compass |
|---|---|
| Two questions: what one thing, done regularly, would make a tremendous difference personally and professionally? | Asked in **onboarding**; the answers become your first long-term goals. |
| Independent will; integrity as honour with yourself | Promises kept (review, Insights); if–then plans for top rocks. |
| Four generations of time management; the fourth manages yourself, relationships and results | The whole app: roles, weekly organizing, people over schedules, P/PC balance through Quadrant II focus and renewal. |
| The time management matrix; importance = contribution to mission, roles and goals; urgency = pressure to act now | Quadrant on every task (`shared/quadrant.ts`): **importance is inferred from a role or goal link, urgency from a deadline**, both overridable. **Matrix** page with an inbox for triage. |
| Q1 consumes crisis managers; Q1 shrinks as Q2 grows | **Prevent it recurring**: turns a Q1 item into a linked Q2 step. Insights: "Where your planned time went", and "Q2 work that became urgent". |
| The first Q2 time comes from Q3 and Q4; say no pleasantly with a bigger yes | In the capture dialog, Q3/Q4 responses show **your bigger yes** (this week's rocks) and log what you declined. Tasks → "Said no". |
| Criteria for a Q2 tool: coherence, balance, Q2 focus, people dimension, flexibility, portability | Design constraints; see [PLAN.md §3](PLAN.md#3-product-principles-the-six-criteria-as-acceptance-tests). |
| Quadrant II organizing: identify roles → select goals → schedule → adapt daily | **Plan your week** ritual and **Week** calendar (rocks tray + 7-day grid); **Today**. |
| Schedule your priorities rather than prioritize your schedule; goals become priorities on a day or, better, appointments | Drag a rock onto a day's **priorities lane** or onto a **time slot**; the capacity meter keeps white space for the unexpected. |
| Sharpen the Saw box on the weekly worksheet | A built-in **Sharpen the Saw** role with four dimension slots each week; coverage in stats and insights. |
| Daily adapting; A/B/C prioritization gives order inside the week's context | **Today** priorities in A · Vital / B · Important / C · Optional groups (drag to reorder), next to the day's schedule. |
| Subordinate the schedule to people with inner peace; no guilt | Moving an item asks *why* ("someone needed me" is a first-class reason); the review counts "I chose a higher value" as integrity kept; planned days never become "overdue". |
| Delegation: gofer vs stewardship; five elements; adjust to maturity | **Stewardships**: agreements with desired results, guidelines, resources, accountability and consequences, plus check-ins (due items appear on Today); Q3 tasks are suggested as candidates. |
| A Q2 approach to a Q1-heavy day (look for the chronic cause, delegate, train) | The Q1 prevention action and Q3 delegation actions on the Matrix and task sheet. |
| Application: estimate your quadrant split, then log three days in 15-minute intervals | **Insights → Time audit**: estimate sliders, a paintable 15-minute grid, estimate vs actual. |
| Application: organize the week, then evaluate how well it translated your values | Review step: rating "How well did your plan turn your deepest values into your days?", wins, lessons. |
| New insights: urgency addiction; 30 minutes of weekly planning; "most important thing in this role this week?" | **Insights → Urgency check** (our own items); a 30-minute timer in the ritual; that question in the Compass step. |
| Sharpen the Saw application: an unmet renewal goal was either set aside for a higher value or an integrity lapse | Exactly the reason choices in the weekly review. |
